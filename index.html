About School

A Caring Preschool Community in Chomu

Shriram Smart Kids is dedicated to building a strong foundation during the most important years of a child’s life. Our learning model blends creativity, structured play, communication skills, and emotional development in a safe and engaging environment.

Located at Krishna Vihar Bhojlawā, Chomu, Jaipur, Rajasthan, we provide a nurturing atmosphere where every child is seen, supported, and inspired to become a confident learner.
